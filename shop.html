Our Story

Born in the Villages of Telangana

In the dusty lanes of rural Telangana, livestock has always been part of life — grazed freely, fed naturally, and raised with care passed down through generations. ANGADI was born to bring this authentic, wholesome produce to your urban kitchen.

We source directly from our own villages and trusted farming families, procuring native-breed goats, country chicken, and cattle raised through traditional free-range and extensive rearing practices. No middlemen. No factory farms. Just the real thing — hygienically processed and delivered fresh to your doorstep.

"We don't sell meat. We deliver the taste your grandmother cooked with."

— Founder, ANGADI
