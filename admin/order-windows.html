Order Windows

← Dashboard
Controls which delivery date customers are ordering for right now — enforced on the website's checkout and the WhatsApp bot. Ordering is open as soon as you add a date, all the way up to its cutoff. Add another one any time — for a normal week, or an extra mid-week delivery — and whichever cutoff comes soonest is the one customers see. All times are IST.
Example: for Sunday delivery, set delivery date = that Sunday and cutoff = whenever you want to stop taking orders (e.g. Saturday 8 PM, or Sunday 10 AM).
