# Angadi: weekly marketing pack (copy, post, repeat)

Goal: more people visiting the site each delivery week. Every link below carries a `?src=` tag, so the daily Growth digest shows which one worked.

## Every delivery week (about 30 minutes)

| When | What | Link to use |
|---|---|---|
| Mon | Post a status on WhatsApp: "This Sunday's fresh stock" | `angadi.farm/?src=wa-status` |
| Tue | Send to 5 apartment / neighbourhood groups you are already in | `angadi.farm/?src=wa-group` |
| Wed | Instagram reel: cutting and weighing in front of the camera | `angadi.farm/?src=insta-bio` (in bio) |
| Thu | Message your past buyers one by one: "Sunday booking open" | `angadi.farm/?src=wa-repeat` |
| Fri | Instagram story with a poll: "Curry cut or boneless?" | `angadi.farm/?src=insta-story` |
| Sat | "Last call, orders close tonight" status and groups | `angadi.farm/?src=wa-lastcall` |
| Sun | Photos of the delivery: packing, cold bag, handover (with customer permission) | |

## Ready-to-send messages

**Status / groups (Mon):**
Fresh village goat meat and country chicken, cut fresh at dawn and delivered cold in Hyderabad this Sunday. Village Pottel mutton ₹1,099/kg. Country chicken ₹900/kg. Order here: angadi.farm/?src=wa-status

**Past buyers (Thu):**
Namaste [name] garu, Sunday booking is open. Same fresh village mutton you ordered last time. Want me to keep your kg ready? Reply here or order: angadi.farm/?src=wa-repeat

**Last call (Sat):**
Last call. Orders for tomorrow close tonight. Fresh mutton and country chicken, delivered Sunday morning. angadi.farm/?src=wa-lastcall

**Someone who left at checkout (use the name and phone from the Growth digest):**
Namaste [name] garu, I saw you were choosing mutton on Angadi. If you had any question about the cut, weight or delivery time, ask me here. Happy to sort it for you.

## Reel ideas (use only what is true; film in real life)

1. "Meet this week's farmer": a short clip with the farmer name and village shown on the homepage.
2. "Cut and weigh in front of you": one kg on the scale, from carcass to pack.
3. "What does ₹1,099 get you?": the full pack and cold bag unboxing.
4. "Curry cut vs biryani cut vs boneless": a 20-second explainer.
5. "Our meat report": show the weight after dressing and the post-slaughter check you already send to customers.

## Trust boosters to collect (never invent these)

- A photo or one-line review from each buyer, with permission.
- A video of the hygiene steps in your facility.
- Your FSSAI number as soon as it is issued.

## Google Business Profile (one-time, 20 minutes)

Create it with the ISB Road, Serilingampally address and a service area of Hyderabad. Add 5 real photos, your delivery-day schedule and the website link `angadi.farm/?src=gbp`. Then ask each buyer for a Google review.
