Live from the Grounds

Watch the herd roam, live

Our goats are reared across Gutrajpalli, Medipalli, Gullakota, Gollapalle, Lingampalli & Kasaram — villages around Jagtial, North Telangana. They aren't penned. Each day they wander out to graze the forest and farmland, doze through the midday heat, and drift home by dusk. This map follows their real, unhurried rhythm — no two days the same.
